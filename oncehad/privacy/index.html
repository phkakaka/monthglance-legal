隐私政策

更新日期：2026年11月12日

数码收藏盒（OnceHad，系统语言为英文时主屏幕名称是 OnceHad）没有账号，也没有开发者自己的服务器。你的收藏记录、设备参数、照片头像和偏好设置只保存在这台设备的本 App 本地空间里，不会上传到开发者这里。

不收集

不注册、不登录、不投放广告、不使用分析工具、不追踪。设备清单、花费与统计全部在本机计算、本机保存。设备库（机型与参数目录）随 App 打包内置，使用时不需要联网。

相机

只有你在编辑某台设备的头像时点「拍照」，App 才会调用相机为这台设备拍摄一张照片作为头像；拍到的照片只保存在本机，不会上传。拒绝相机权限后，你仍可以从相册选择照片或继续使用默认图标，不影响其他功能。

照片

只有你为某台设备设置照片头像、或选择海报配图时，才经由系统的照片选择器读取你选中的那几张照片，照片副本保存在本机。不会读取你未选择的照片，也不会在后台上传。

相册写入

只有你点「保存到相册」导出海报时，才把生成的海报图片写入系统相册。拒绝后，仍可使用分享面板把海报存到「文件」或其他 App。

备份与恢复

「导出收藏盒数据」会生成一个备份包（ZIP），由你自行通过系统分享面板保存或发送；「导入」只读取你主动选择的备份包。这个过程中开发者拿不到、也保存不了任何数据。

购买

OnceHad Pro 是一次买断。付款由应用商店的应用内购买系统处理——App 仅在你购买或恢复购买时连接该服务，开发者收不到银行卡或账号信息。买断状态只记在这台设备的本地空间里，跟你的商店账号走，换机可在 App 内恢复。

可在系统设置中随时关闭本 App 的相机与照片相关权限。本政策不面向收集儿童信息。

Privacy Policy

Updated: 12 November 2026

OnceHad has no account and no developer-run server. Your collection records, device specs, photo avatars and preferences live only in the app's local storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded to the developer.

No collection

No sign-up, no login, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Device lists, spending and statistics are computed and stored on-device. The device library (model and spec catalog) ships inside the app and works offline.

Camera

Only when you tap "Take Photo" while editing a device's avatar does the app invoke the camera to shoot a photo for that device; the photo stays on this device and is never uploaded. If you decline camera access, you can still pick a photo from the library or keep the default artwork — every other feature keeps working.

Photos

Only when you set a photo avatar for a device or pick a poster image does the app read the selected photos through the system photo picker; copies stay on this device. Photos you did not select are never read, and nothing is uploaded in the background.

Writing to Photos

Only when you tap "Save to Photos" does the app write the generated poster into your photo library. If you decline, you can still save posters to Files or other apps via the share sheet.

Backup & restore

"Export box data" builds a ZIP backup that you save or send yourself through the system share sheet; "Import" reads only the backup file you pick. The developer never receives or stores this data.

Purchases

OnceHad Pro is a one-time purchase handled by the app store's in-app purchase system — the app connects to it only when you buy or restore, and the developer never sees your card or account details. The purchase state lives on this device and follows your store account; restore it on a new device inside the app.

You can revoke the camera and photo-related permissions for this app in system settings at any time. This policy does not address collection of children's information.
