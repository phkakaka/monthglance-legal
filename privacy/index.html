隐私政策

更新日期：2026年10月18日

月览日历（系统语言为英文时，主屏幕名称是 DateMap）没有账号，也没有开发者自己的服务器。本政策同时适用于 iOS 版（DateMap）与 HarmonyOS 版（月览日历 / MonthGlance），两版行为一致，个别系统服务不同，会在下文分别说明。

不收集

不注册、不登录、不投放广告、不使用分析工具、不追踪。日历日程、倒数日、颜色和设置保存在这台设备和本 App 与桌面插件共用的本地空间里，不会上传到开发者这里。

日历

用来显示、创建和编辑系统日历中的日程。卸载本 App 会删除本 App 的设置、倒数日和回收站；已经写入系统日历的日程仍由系统日历保存。

通知

可选。只在你打开提醒时，由本 App 经系统的提醒/通知通道发本地通知（HarmonyOS 版使用系统代理提醒服务）。拒绝通知后，仍可浏览和编辑日程。

位置

只有你在编辑日程时点位置行左边的十字，才申请「使用 App 期间」的定位，读取一次当前位置并填入地点。不会在后台持续跟踪。拒绝后仍可手打地点，或用放大镜搜索地点。HarmonyOS 版申请的是大致位置，同样只在点十字时读取一次。

地点查询经由系统服务

点十字把坐标换成地址，或用放大镜搜索地点时，这一次查询会交给系统地图/定位服务处理（iOS 上是 Apple 的地图服务，HarmonyOS 上是系统的位置服务），只为把结果填回这一行。开发者拿不到、也不保存这份网络记录。地点文字和坐标写进该条日程，存在系统日历里。

购买

月览 Pro（iOS 上叫 DateMap Pro）是一次买断。付款由对应平台的系统内购处理：iOS 上由 Apple 处理，HarmonyOS 上由华为应用内支付处理——App 仅在你购买或恢复购买时连接该支付服务，开发者收不到银行卡或账号信息。iOS 上曾购买旧「组合」桌面组件的用户已自动拥有 Pro；两平台的买断状态都只记在这台设备与桌面组件共用的本地空间里。

可在系统设置中关闭本 App 的日历、通知或定位权限。本政策不面向收集儿童信息。

Privacy Policy

Updated: 18 October 2026

DateMap (shown as 月览日历 when the system language is Chinese) has no account and no server operated by the developer. This policy covers both the iOS version (DateMap) and the HarmonyOS version (MonthGlance). The two behave the same; a few system services differ, and each is noted below.

What we do not collect

There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Events, countdowns, colors, and settings stay on this device, in storage shared by the app and its widgets. They are not uploaded to the developer.

Calendars

Calendars are used to show, create, and edit events in the system Calendar. Deleting the app removes its settings, countdowns, and trash. Events already written to Calendar remain in Calendar.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. The app sends a local notification only when you turn a reminder on (the HarmonyOS version uses the system agent-powered reminder service). Denying notifications does not block browsing or editing.

Location

Location is requested only when you tap the crosshair on an event’s place row, and only while the app is in use. It reads your location once to fill that place. It is not tracked in the background. If you deny it, you can still type a place or search with the magnifying glass. The HarmonyOS version requests approximate location, likewise read only once per tap.

Place lookup goes through system services

Turning a coordinate into an address, or searching for a place, sends that one query to the system’s map or location service (Apple’s map service on iOS, the system location service on HarmonyOS) so the result can be written into the field. The developer does not receive or store that network record. The place text and coordinate are saved on the event in Calendar.

Purchases

Pro (DateMap Pro on iOS, 月览 Pro on HarmonyOS) is a one-time purchase. Payment is processed by the platform’s system purchase service — Apple on iOS, Huawei In-App Purchases on HarmonyOS; the app connects to that service only when you buy or restore. The developer does not receive your card or account details. On iOS, buyers of the old Combo widget already have Pro. On both platforms the unlock flag stays on this device, in storage shared with the widgets.

You can turn off Calendar, Notifications, or Location for this app in system Settings. This policy is not used to collect information from children.
