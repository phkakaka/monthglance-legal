隐私政策

更新日期：2026年9月22日

月览日历（系统语言为英文时，主屏幕名称是 DateMap）没有账号，也没有开发者自己的服务器。

不收集

不注册、不登录、不投放广告、不使用分析工具、不追踪。日历日程、倒数日、颜色和设置保存在这台设备和本 App 与桌面插件共用的本地空间里，不会上传到开发者这里。

日历

用来显示、创建和编辑系统日历中的日程。卸载本 App 会删除本 App 的设置、倒数日和回收站；已经写入系统日历的日程仍由系统日历保存。

通知

可选。只在你打开提醒时，由本 App 发本地通知。拒绝通知后，仍可浏览和编辑日程。

位置

只有你在编辑日程时点位置行左边的十字，才申请「使用 App 期间」的定位，读取一次当前位置并填入地点。不会在后台持续跟踪。拒绝后仍可手打地点，或用放大镜搜索地点。

地点查询会经过 Apple

点十字把坐标换成地址，或用放大镜搜索地点时，这一次查询会发给 Apple 的地图服务，只为把结果填回这一行。开发者拿不到、也不保存这份网络记录。地点文字和坐标写进该条日程，存在系统日历里。

购买

「组合」桌面组件是一次买断。付款由 Apple 处理。开发者不收到银行卡或 Apple ID。买断状态只记在这台设备与桌面组件共用的本地空间里。其他桌面组件不收费。

可在 iOS「设置」中关闭本 App 的日历、通知或定位权限。本政策不面向收集儿童信息。

Privacy Policy

Updated: 22 September 2026

DateMap (shown as 月览日历 when the system language is Chinese) has no account and no server operated by the developer.

What we do not collect

There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Events, countdowns, colors, and settings stay on this device, in storage shared by the app and its widgets. They are not uploaded to the developer.

Calendars

Calendars are used to show, create, and edit events in the system Calendar. Deleting the app removes its settings, countdowns, and trash. Events already written to Calendar remain in Calendar.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. The app sends a local notification only when you turn a reminder on. Denying notifications does not block browsing or editing.

Location

Location is requested only when you tap the crosshair on an event’s place row, and only while the app is in use. It reads your location once to fill that place. It is not tracked in the background. If you deny it, you can still type a place or search with the magnifying glass.

Place lookup uses Apple

Turning a coordinate into an address, or searching for a place, sends that one query to Apple’s map service so the result can be written into the field. The developer does not receive or store that network record. The place text and coordinate are saved on the event in Calendar.

Purchases

The Combo widget is a one-time purchase. Apple processes the payment. The developer does not receive your card or Apple ID. The unlock flag stays on this device, in storage shared with the widget. Other widgets are free.

You can turn off Calendar, Notifications, or Location for this app in iOS Settings. This policy is not used to collect information from children.
